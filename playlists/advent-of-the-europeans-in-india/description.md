## Playlist description (paste into YouTube)

In 1498 a Portuguese fleet anchored off Calicut. Over the next four and a half centuries, five European powers came to India to trade. Only one ended up ruling it.

This animated series tells the story of each one: who came first, who fought whom, who quietly sold up and went home, and how a London trading company ended up governing a subcontinent. Every episode runs about 5–7 minutes, with animated maps, characters and sourced facts.

▶ Watch in order:

1. First In, Last Out: The Portuguese in India (1498–1961)
   Vasco da Gama reaches Calicut, Goa falls in 1510, and Portugal holds on until 1961.

2. Traders, Not Rulers: The Dutch in India (1596–1825)
   The spice giant that chose profit over power, and lost its last Indian posts in 1825.

3. From Merchants to Masters: The English in India, 1600–1947
   From a royal charter in 1600 to a company that ruled India, and then the Crown.

4. Hero or Survivor? The Last Dane in India
   A small Danish outpost at Tranquebar that outlasted far bigger rivals, until 1845.

5. How France Almost Won India (and Executed Its Own General)
   France's bid for India, the rivalry with Britain, and why Paris beheaded Lally in 1766.

## Notes

- **Playlist order:** set it to the order above (oldest arrival first): Portuguese → Dutch → English → Danes → French. Change it under *Playlist settings → Ordering → Manual*, then drag the videos.
- **Facts used** were checked against Wikipedia (Portuguese India, Dutch India, Danish India, French India, Comte de Lally):
  - Vasco da Gama reached Calicut on 20 May 1498.
  - Goa was taken in 1510, and the Indian annexation came in December 1961.
  - The Dutch lost their last Indian trading posts in 1825.
  - The Danish company was chartered in 1616, Tranquebar was acquired in 1620, and it was sold to Britain in 1845.
  - The French company was re-founded under Colbert in 1664.
  - Lally was executed in May 1766.
- **Years on the thumbnail** are each power's start year. The Portuguese (1498) and Dutch (1596) years are arrival dates. The English (1600), Danish (1616) and French (1664) years are company charter dates. These match the episode titles.
- **Flags** are simplified period designs:
  - Portugal: a white flag with a blue shield.
  - The Dutch: the orange, white and blue Prince's Flag.
  - The English East India Company: red and white stripes with a St George canton.
  - Denmark: the Dannebrog.
  - France: a white Bourbon flag with gold lilies.
- **Map pins** are placed at the real coordinates of Surat, Goa, Pulicat, Pondicherry and Tranquebar, on Natural Earth coastlines. The map carries a `MAP SIMPLIFIED` tag.
- **To edit the thumbnail,** change `thumbnail-source/make.mjs`, then re-render. Running it needs `world-atlas`, `topojson-client`, `d3-geo` and the `@fontsource` packages for Bebas Neue, EB Garamond and Inter.
